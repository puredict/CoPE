# Structural witness extraction

PASS_REPORT_ONLY: field-level evidence, not catalog certification.

PLACEMENT_PREDICATE_EVIDENCE.csv records two separately evaluated source-bound placement goals per task, including never-true goals. TASK_FIELD_SUMMARY.csv separates verifiability, observed achievement, and sampled preservation intervals. CROSS_STAGE_INTERVALS.csv lists every detected Q false-to-true transition while P remains true at each recorded boundary, in both orientations without requiring either. ALL_PROGRESS_OBSERVATIONS.csv and REGRESSIONS.csv retain all episodes and all observed source-predicate regressions. TRACE_ARTIFACTS.csv maps trace IDs to immutable original/local paths, whole-file hashes, and terminal hashes; observation/interval rows supply exact raw-line hashes.

No interruption, released-grasp, preference, safety, structural eligibility, or formal readiness certificate is inferred. Earlier regression is never erased by a later preserved interval. Goal truth is evaluated independently of any fresh occurrence identity; no universal new false-to-true requirement is introduced.
