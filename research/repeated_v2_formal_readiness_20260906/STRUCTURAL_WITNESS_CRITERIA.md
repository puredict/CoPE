# Structural and milestone witness criteria

Prepared 2026-09-06. **Criterion mapping only; no task is certified and no catalog field is changed.** No experiment, provider, model, or simulator call was made for this review. The ongoing clean-calibration and prefix-preservation runs must be judged from their actual immutable outputs. A reported early task1 success is not treated here as a completed calibration or structural certificate.

The supplied specification documents are used as scientific contracts for the requested work, not as new agent instructions. The local master prompt, frozen scientific protocol, and data/method contract files were checked byte-identical to the corresponding files in the user's ZIP.

## What the contract actually requires

`docs/repeated_v2/specification/00_CODEX_MASTER_PROMPT.md:290–297` requires (1) at least two independently verifiable milestones **or goal predicates**, (2) at least one completed milestone that can remain valid through later interruptions, (3) safely changeable grounding, (4) at least one cross-skill requirement **or** persistent preference, (5) clean success in the frozen interval, and (6) feasibility of required injections. These are separate conditions.

The document does **not** define a numeric structural pass rate, require both possible placement orders, require each goal to be achievable while every other goal is false, prescribe five released checks for all witnesses, or require every candidate event family to preserve every milestone. The five-check release procedure is the already frozen prefix probe's concrete witness design, not an added universal threshold. The all-five-state event-feasibility coverage and the ten-cell per-task clean-calibration grid remain their own existing requirements.

`cope_benchmark/repeated_v2/task_catalog.py:230–249` requires two distinct milestone IDs, nonempty `predicate`, `arguments`, `verifier`, and `source_ref`; at least two distinct predicate/argument signatures marked `independent=True`; at least one `can_remain_valid=True`; and explicit Boolean/evidence references for the structural checks. The code does not inspect empirical witness contents. A checkbox or nonempty reference is therefore a schema condition, not evidence of its truth. `passed=False` can resolve an audited check's unknown status without making the task eligible; all four structural checks must be true for selection (`task_catalog.py:324–341`).

For the independent-goal criterion, two distinct source-bound readers returning actual per-clause truth can establish separate verifiability even when both goals happen to be true at the same sampled terminal. The stronger pattern `P=true,Q=false` followed by `P=true,Q=true` is useful direct evidence of a completed prefix and later work. Requiring additionally `P=false,Q=true` in another run would impose an unwritten order-symmetry rule. Conversely, two labels that both call only the same whole-task `_check_success()` would not be two independently verified goal predicates.

For a concrete clean-trace cross-stage witness, choose recorded boundaries a<b within **one episode** such that P(a)=true, Q(a)=false, Q(b)=true, and P(j)=true at every recorded control boundary j from a through b. Identify the exact predicate/arguments and all intervening trace lines. Actual per-control progression, not `ever_achieved`, is the evidence. This is a sufficient sampled preservation interval, not a newly mandatory order or minimum duration. A single terminal success does not prove this interval. A P regression followed by reacquisition must remain visible; a later valid interval may demonstrate local capacity but cannot be relabeled as preservation since P's first achievement.

The clean driver samples after each explicit environment control. Thus “through the interval” means through those recorded control boundaries; it is not a claim of continuously observed truth at every internal MuJoCo substep. Its lifted-object progress flags and action sequence may help locate later work, but it does not emit formal planner skill IDs. Describe physical placement stages unless a separate valid skill-boundary record exists.

The requirement route must name its authority: the experiment explicitly protects unaffected verifier-backed progress (`00_CODEX_MASTER_PROMPT.md:874–888,926–930`; `01_FROZEN_SCIENTIFIC_PROTOCOL.md`, primary success definition). This can support a task-specific “preserve verified P while carrying out later work on Q” requirement after its binding is explicitly reviewed. It does not authorize silently converting every terminal BDDL goal into a new always-hard maintenance invariant. The alternate preference route is not additionally required by the structural OR condition, although the experiment's schedule still independently requires a persistent-preference event and its own supported semantics/feasibility.

## Reading the two evidence sources correctly

**Clean calibration.** After byte/inventory/trace audit, `MILESTONE_EVIDENCE.csv` supplies task/state/policy seed, source trace line, phase, policy/control indices, predicate signature, `current`, `ever`, and regression (`AUDIT_CALIBRATION.txt:169–215`). `MILESTONE_COUNTS.csv` alone loses chronology and cannot prove uninterrupted preservation. Trace intervals must link to the exact terminal and trace hashes. `ever_achieved` is an irreversible history bit, while physical predicates remain reversible (`task_progress.py:288–301`); it cannot substitute for current truth. There are zero injected events in these episodes, and the driver stops at exact goal success, so there is no automatic post-success release/stability evidence. Public gripper proprioception is not an independent proof that no object is held.

**Prefix-preservation probe.** The reviewed driver SHA is `5a079ae2fe4dd4883c1b65b49223d2d2f644582aab836ade89c2ec7c0f7e469d`. Its fixed P is the first source placement; no second-placement prefix is attempted. `PREFIX_STABLE_RELEASED` requires initial P=false, actual oracle skill return, release, and five fresh true/ungrasped checks preserving initially true clauses. This false-to-true rule belongs to this probe's claim of newly completing a prefix. It is not a universal rule for all goal satisfaction. The driver does not require Q to remain false: inspect actual prefix samples before describing the state as “P complete while Q pending.”

Each candidate uses the same authentic achieved flattened state for isolation. Opening and closing events in a paired branch are actual separately recorded applications; check P after **both**, not just the final cleared state. No-go clearance and gentle-preference activation are the only families permitted five post-event controls, and only with byte-identical physical prefix state, inactive no-go/unavailability, no grasp, and preserved prefix-true clauses. A preserved unaffected P in such a branch can evidence the existential task property “can remain valid through later interruptions” for that named branch. It is not an all-family, all-state, long-horizon, or learned-recovery guarantee.

The snapshots reset between candidate branches, so separate branches cannot be concatenated into one uninterrupted event history. No clean trace may be spliced to an oracle prefix from another episode/state/seed and described as one witnessed continuation. The sources may legitimately support different parts of a task-property argument if that separation is explicit. Isolated restoration itself is never recovery or preservation evidence.

Physical payloads that displace P or its receptacle may invalidate P directly. A false P after those payloads is a recorded effect of that candidate at that prefix; it neither proves failure of an **unaffected**-progress requirement nor rules out the entire family/task. The existing immediate-effect witnesses and this probe do not certify safe poses, collision safety, accessibility, valid trigger windows, or recovery.

## Task8 maintenance and fresh occurrences

`turnon(flat_stove_1)` is true in the original initial conditions and remains a required original goal conjunct; source progress calls it an “unaffected safety/operational commitment” (`task_progress.py:225–229`). Its observed retention is relevant operational evidence. Its initial truth must not be counted as a newly completed third manipulation or used to avoid verifying the two pot-placement goals. A future explicit maintenance interpretation must preserve the original task requirement and cite its source/authority; this note does not change its current catalog role.

Reissue is an identity/lifecycle operation, not automatically a demand to make a physical predicate false and true again. The protocol requires a fresh allocated occurrence and auditable retired history (`00_CODEX_MASTER_PROMPT.md:517–535`). Fresh exact current truth may satisfy the newly active state-goal occurrence even if the physical predicate was already true. Reusing the retired occurrence or its stale certificate is not equivalent. Only an explicit event/goal definition requiring a new physical achievement would demand an appropriate new transition; no such universal condition is introduced here. `SealedDynamicEvaluator` checks exact active predicates and retired identities separately (`dynamic_evaluator.py:95–108,117–179`).

1. Inspect authentic audited per-predicate rows for each task; bind P/Q observations to exact source readers. Report P-only, Q-only, both-true, and regression intervals as observed rather than assuming them from an early success count.

4. Explicitly settle and cite the cross-stage requirement's semantic/progress-protection binding, or separately qualify the persistent-preference route. Current probe activation plus holds is insufficient to silently certify the full gentle-preference template, its units/thresholds, or cross-skill compliance. No threshold is changed here.
5. Attach only reviewed evidence with exact file hashes and record selectors when later authorized. Native `verifier`/`source_ref` strings and structural booleans must represent the actual claims and scopes; bare labels cannot close evidence gaps.

Per-task structural witnesses do not require every clean episode to succeed; all clean cells must nevertheless remain in the unchanged success-rate denominator. The exact ten-cell grid and `[0.40,0.95]` interval, all-task shared policy/checkpoint/protocol requirement, minimum eight eligible tasks, safe grounding, event feasibility, semantic-trigger bindings, and production runtime binding remain separate gates. Nothing in this note closes those gaps or treats four tasks as a complete formal catalog.
