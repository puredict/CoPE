# ICRA-style reviewer simulation after novelty audit

Date: 2026-08-04

## Current recommendation

**Weak reject in the current state.**  The method question is well isolated and
the experimental protocol is unusually careful, but the decisive learned
comparison has not run.  Existing evidence establishes substrate correctness,
not that CoPE adds value.  The broad local-repair novelty language also collides
with InstructFlow and classical/LLM plan-repair work.

If the locked decisive gate passes but no new task is added, the likely rating
remains borderline/weak reject for ICRA: one LIBERO task identity with a
privileged oracle skill controller is too narrow for a general robot-recovery
claim.  A tie/loss against generic JSON patch should trigger the assurance-
framework reframe rather than a method-superiority submission.

## Major objections and required evidence

### 1. "Targeted symbolic repair already exists"

InstructFlow performs typed symbolic, constraint-induced targeted repair without
full regeneration; plan-analysis work explicitly repairs instead of replanning.
The paper must claim persistent commitment transaction semantics, not local
repair itself.  Required: a full related-work dimension table and no unsupported
"first" language.

### 2. "The typed vocabulary may be cosmetic"

A smaller `Override`/`Expire` object may merely be easier for the model to emit
than generic JSON.  Required: the frozen CoPE-versus-generic-JSON primary
comparison, common input/prefix hashes, proposal-byte reporting, and an ablation
that separates operation vocabulary from trusted authorization/receipt/hash
enforcement.  Be prepared for the result that the assurance layer—not the typed
tokens—is the contribution.

### 3. "The experiment is one task with an oracle controller"

Two prefix orientations are not two task identities.  The low-level controller
has privileged object/skill access, so results concern high-level commitment
state, not perception or visuomotor recovery.  Required: at least one fresh
physically feasible multi-object task; preferably real-robot or non-oracle
execution.  Otherwise narrow every claim and title accordingly.

### 4. "Baselines may be project-defined straw men"

FSR-PC and full-replan schemas are operationalizations, not automatically
canonical published algorithms.  Required: define the capability class rather
than borrowing authority from a name; show equal observations, call budgets,
model, seeds and trusted materialization; cite the closest actual regeneration
and replanning systems.  The generic JSON path arm is the primary adversary and
must remain prominent even if secondary baselines lose badly.

### 5. "Forty pairs and one model may not generalize"

The exact paired test is valid but measures one frozen model/provider protocol.
The observed discordance and confidence interval must be reported, including
null results.  Required after a positive primary result: a separately
preregistered replication with another capable model or local model, without
retuning the original prompt from formal outcomes.

### 6. "Interruption recovery is narrower than failure recovery"

The locked events are owner-authorized goal replacement/cancellation after
completed progress.  They do not cover grasp failure, collision, perception
error or arbitrary environment change.  Required: consistently say user/task
interruption or commitment revision; do not generalize to all execution failure
recovery.  Physical failures are substrate exclusions, not CoPE events.

### 7. "Submission decision still depends on hidden researcher judgment"

This risk is substantially reduced by the frozen 40/40 substrate, +15 pp,
McNemar, no-excess safety and locality gates.  Required: publish every frozen
artifact/hash, all failure cells and the exact 6--0 / 8--1 decision boundary;
do not replace the combined gate with a favorable secondary metric.

## Evidence ladder

1. **Now:** technical readiness only; do not submit the method claim.
2. **Smoke PASS:** interface compatibility only; still do not claim efficacy.
3. **Formal gate FAIL:** assurance-framework reframe or stop.
4. **Formal gate PASS, one task:** controlled proof-of-concept; likely below
   broad ICRA empirical expectations.
5. **Formal PASS + fresh task/model replication:** credible ICRA submission
   candidate, provided novelty language stays at transaction semantics.
6. **Add non-oracle/real robot:** strongest response to PLanAR/InstructFlow
   empirical-scope objections.
