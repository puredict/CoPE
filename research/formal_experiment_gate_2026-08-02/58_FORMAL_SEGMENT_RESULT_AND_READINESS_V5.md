## What can and cannot be claimed

Supported claim:

> For interruption families that modify or cancel pending commitments,
> generating a small persistent-state edit is substantially more reliable than
> regenerating the whole semantic state, and the validated edit can drive the
> same physical recovery as a neutral sparse interface.

Unsupported claims:

- that `Override`/`Expire` names themselves beat neutral operators;
- a completed 40-case confirmatory embodied result;
- learned visuomotor recovery, because the low-level controller is privileged;
- broad robot/task generality beyond the current task and event families.

## Submission judgment

The idea is no longer a speculative dead end: it has independent holdout,
scaling, learned high-level integration, exact matched physical traces, cold
provenance and a six-state reserved replication. The publishable contribution
should be framed as **persistent commitment-state editing / sparse recovery
interfaces**, with CoPE as the concrete typed realization.

For a full ICRA/CoRL/RSS-strength empirical claim, one clean confirmatory
embodied study and greater task/event diversity are still needed. Submitting now
would invite a decisive reviewer objection: the confirmatory run stopped on a
shared controller failure, and the strongest physical evidence is one task.
